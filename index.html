    How to go bankrupt

    Be the first player with $0 and no stocks. Cash stops at zero.

    1. Roll and draw. Both happen automatically. The two dice set the stock price at per pip. A draw pile with fewer cards than players gets topped up with the shuffled discards.
    2. Play up to one card. Lose cards target yourself, Gain cards can target anyone, and Action cards follow their own text.
    3. Trade. Buy only if your cash is strictly more than twice the price before each purchase. You can hold up to five stocks. Sell any number at the current price.
    4. End your turn. Collect per stock in dividends, then shuffle any cards over seven back into the main deck.

    Golden Toilet. The first player to reach or more resets to and keeps their stocks. It only flushes once per game.
